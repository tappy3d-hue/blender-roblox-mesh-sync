Roblox Material preview library

Source: Roblox Studio OBJ material export supplied for this project on 2026-08-14.
Contents: 43 material folders, Wavefront MTL files, and their referenced PNG maps.
Projection scale: one texture repetition per 8 Roblox studs, based on the exported OBJ UVs.

Only files referenced by the material folders are bundled. The source export's
duplicate reports, unknown-file folders, and sample OBJ geometry are not needed
at runtime and are not included in the Blender extension.
